# Day 01 – Introduction to DevOps and Cloud
What is your understanding of DevOps and Cloud Engineering?

DevOps is a culture and mindset that focuses on bringing the Development team (Dev) and the Operations team (Ops) together.
The goal is to reduce the time to market, automate repetitive tasks, and deliver software faster and more reliably.

Example 1: Pizza Shop

In a pizza shop:

Chef makes the pizza

Delivery boy delivers it to the customer

If they don’t coordinate:

Pizza becomes cold

Delivery is late

Incorrect order is delivered

But if they communicate constantly:

Chef knows which order is urgent

Delivery boy knows when pizza is ready

Customer gets hot pizza on time
Dev = Chef
Ops = Delivery boy
DevOps = Perfect teamwork + Good communication + No delays
